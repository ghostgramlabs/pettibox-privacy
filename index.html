Privacy Policy for PettiBox

Effective Date: May 25, 2026

PettiBox is developed by GhostGram Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how PettiBox handles information when you use the app.

1. Overview

PettiBox is an offline-first Android app that helps you save links, notes, images, screenshots, PDFs, text, and files so you can organize, search, and find them later.

PettiBox does not require an account and does not operate a backend server for storing your saved content.

2. Information You Save in the App

PettiBox stores the content you choose to save, which may include:

  • Links and URLs
  • Text, notes, and selected text shared from other apps
  • Images and screenshots
  • PDFs and files
  • Titles, tags, collections, notes, and reminders you add
  • Text extracted from images or PDFs for search
  • Link metadata such as page titles, descriptions, and preview images

This information is stored locally on your device in PettiBox app storage unless you choose to share, export, back up, or restore it.

3. Text Recognition / OCR

PettiBox may scan images and PDFs to extract text so your saved items can be searched inside the app.

PettiBox uses text recognition functionality, including Google ML Kit text recognition, to help process saved images and documents for searchable text.

You can turn off automatic text recognition in PettiBox Settings. When it is turned off, new images and PDFs will still be saved, but PettiBox will not automatically extract searchable text from them.

4. Link Metadata and Thumbnails

When you save a link, PettiBox may connect to that webpage to fetch metadata such as the page title, description, and preview image. This helps display a useful preview inside the app.

When PettiBox contacts a webpage for metadata, the website you saved may receive standard connection information such as your IP address and user-agent. PettiBox does not use this to track you.

5. Reminders and Notifications

If you set a reminder for a saved item, PettiBox stores the reminder time on your device and uses Android alarms and notifications to remind you.

PettiBox may request notification permission so it can show reminder notifications. PettiBox may also use Android alarm permissions to deliver reminders closer to the time you choose.

PettiBox may listen for device reboot, time, or timezone changes so it can re-schedule reminders that should still fire. Reminder data is not sent to a GhostGram Labs server.

6. Permissions

PettiBox may request or use the following permissions and Android features:

  • Internet: Used to fetch link metadata, page titles, and preview images for saved links.
  • Notifications: Used only to show reminder notifications you set in PettiBox.
  • Exact alarm / alarm scheduling: Used to deliver reminders at or near the time you choose.
  • Run after reboot: Used to re-schedule pending reminders after a device restart or time change.
  • Media / image access: Used only when you choose images or screenshots to save into PettiBox.
  • File access through Android picker or share sheet: Used only when you choose to save files, PDFs, images, or other shared content.
  • Folder access for backups: Used only if you choose a folder where PettiBox should copy backup files.

7. Sharing, Exporting, and File Access

PettiBox lets you share individual saved items, attachments, or export a backup file. When you choose to share or export content, that content is sent to the app or service you select, such as email, cloud storage, or a messaging app.

PettiBox is not responsible for how third-party apps or services handle content after you choose to share or export it.

8. Backup Files and Restore

PettiBox can create backup files containing your saved data, including saved items, collections, attachments, tags, reminders, archive/favorite/pinned status, extracted text, and related metadata.

PettiBox may create automatic local safety copies if you enable automatic backup in Settings. These backups are stored on your device in PettiBox's backup folder, and PettiBox keeps recent copies to help avoid excessive storage use.

If you choose a backup folder, PettiBox may also copy backup files to that folder. You are responsible for where you store, copy, upload, or share backup files.

On a fresh install or empty app state, PettiBox may check its own backup folder for a PettiBox backup and ask whether you want to restore it. PettiBox only checks its own backup folder automatically. If your backup is somewhere else, you can manually choose a backup file from Settings.

9. Archive and Deletion

PettiBox includes Archive as a safety net. Archived items remain stored on your device and may remain searchable or recoverable inside the app.

When you permanently delete saved items or attachments, PettiBox removes the corresponding local database records and local attachment files where applicable.

Backup files you previously exported, shared, copied, or stored separately are not automatically deleted when you delete content from PettiBox.

10. Data Collection

PettiBox does not collect your saved content on a GhostGram Labs server. PettiBox does not require login or account registration.

PettiBox does not sell your personal information.

11. Third-Party Services and Libraries

PettiBox uses third-party Android libraries and services to provide app functionality, including image loading, local database storage, background processing, file handling, link preview parsing, and text recognition.

PettiBox may use Google ML Kit text recognition to process images and PDFs for searchable text.

12. Data Retention

Saved content remains on your device until you archive it, delete it, export it, restore over it, or uninstall PettiBox.

Uninstalling PettiBox removes app-local data from your device, subject to Android system behavior and any backup files you created, copied, exported, or shared separately.

PettiBox disables Android's automatic app-data backup for its private app data. PettiBox backup files are created only through PettiBox's own backup features.

13. Children’s Privacy

PettiBox is not intended for children under 13. GhostGram Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

14. Security

PettiBox stores saved content locally on your device using Android app storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but PettiBox is designed to keep your saved content local unless you choose to share, export, back up, or restore it.

Backup files may contain saved content and attachments. You should store backup files somewhere you trust.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, the updated policy will be made available with a new effective date.
