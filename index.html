Privacy Policy for PettiBox

Effective Date: May 10, 2026

PettiBox is developed by GhostGram Labs. This Privacy Policy explains how PettiBox handles information when you use the app.

1. Overview

PettiBox is an offline-first Android app that helps you save links, notes, images, screenshots, PDFs, text, and files so you can organize and search them later.

PettiBox does not require an account and does not operate a backend server for storing your saved content.

2. Information You Save in the App

PettiBox stores the content you choose to save, which may include:

  • Links and URLs
  • Text and notes
  • Images and screenshots
  • PDFs and files
  • Titles, tags, categories, and notes you add
  • Text extracted from images or PDFs for search

This information is stored locally on your device in the app’s private storage unless you choose to share or export it.

3. Text Recognition / OCR

PettiBox may scan images and PDFs to extract text so that your saved items can be searched. This text recognition is used to improve search inside the app.

You can turn off automatic text recognition in the app’s Settings. When it is turned off, new images and PDFs will still be saved, but PettiBox will not extract searchable text from them.

4. Link Metadata and Thumbnails

When you save a link, PettiBox may connect to that webpage to fetch metadata such as the page title, description, and preview image. This helps display a useful preview inside the app.

When PettiBox contacts a webpage for metadata, the website you saved may receive standard connection information such as your IP address and browser user-agent. PettiBox does not use this to track you.

5. Permissions

PettiBox may request or use the following permissions:

  • Internet: Used to fetch link metadata, page titles, and preview images for saved links.
  • Media / Images access: Used only when you choose images or screenshots to save into PettiBox.
  • File access through Android picker/share sheet: Used only when you choose to save files, PDFs, images, or other shared content.

6. Sharing and Exporting

PettiBox lets you share individual saved items, attachments, or export a backup file. When you choose to share or export content, that content is sent to the app or service you select, such as email, cloud storage, or a messaging app.

PettiBox is not responsible for how third-party apps or services handle content after you choose to share or export it.

7. Backup Files

PettiBox can create a JSON backup file containing your saved data, such as saved items, categories, attachments, tags, and related metadata. Backup is started only when you choose to export it.

You are responsible for where you store or send the backup file.

8. Data Collection

PettiBox does not collect your saved content on a GhostGram Labs server. PettiBox does not require login or account registration.

PettiBox does not sell your personal information.

9. Third-Party Services and Libraries

PettiBox uses third-party Android libraries and services to provide app functionality, including image loading, local database storage, background processing, and text recognition.

PettiBox may use Google ML Kit text recognition to process images and PDFs for searchable text.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Saved content remains on your device until you delete it from the app or uninstall PettiBox.

You can delete saved items and attachments inside the app. Uninstalling the app removes app-local data from your device, subject to Android system behavior and any backups you created or shared separately.

11. Children’s Privacy

PettiBox is not intended for children under 13. GhostGram Labs does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

12. Security

PettiBox stores saved content locally on your device using Android app storage. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but PettiBox is designed to keep your saved content local unless you choose to share or export it.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, the updated policy will be made available with a new effective date.
